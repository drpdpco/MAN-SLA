For students and teachers

Using AI the right way

AI is a study partner here, not a ghost-writer. These rules apply to every activity above, and the declaration and prompt journal are part of every submission.

Do your own work first

Read, watch, observe or draft before opening an AI tool. Then use AI to check, question and improve what you made.

Keep a prompt journal

Record each prompt, what the AI answered, and what you kept or changed. Screenshots are fine.

Verify every fact

AI tools can invent dates, clauses, numbers and quotes. Check each one against a book, the standard, or an official website.

Protect people's data

Never paste names, phone numbers, marks or confidential company data of others. Anonymise survey and feedback data first.

Declare your AI use

Attach the AI-use declaration below. Using AI openly is allowed. Hiding it is not.

Your words, your analysis

AI-written text submitted as your own is not accepted. Conclusions and reflections must be in your own words.
